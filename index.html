DatesDaysWhere and why

How this is counted

  • A “hospital day” is any calendar day he was in a hospital at any point, counting the admission and discharge days. Same-day visits for exams or procedures count as one day.
  • An open stay counts up to today (Brasília time) until a discharge date is added.
  • Not counted: the many prison infirmary and home medical visits reported in early 2026, a July 2021 dental procedure, and a kidney-stone procedure some outlets place in 2019 without a date.
  • Reports sometimes disagree on details (e.g. which procedures were done in Sept 2023 and Dec 2025); the table notes this where it matters. Corrections welcome via the repository.
